# Code of Conduct

HydroDock should be a friendly, respectful project for people learning and building embedded systems.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming and patient.
- Assume good intent.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and constraints.
- Help keep discussions useful and on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Dismissive comments toward beginners or people asking questions.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that makes the project less welcoming, please contact the maintainer through the repository issue tracker or the contact method listed in the project profile. Maintainers should review reports promptly and act in the best interest of a safe, constructive community.
